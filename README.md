## 💫 About Me

📊 I'm a **Data Engineer and Business Intelligence Engineer** who loves taking data from raw source to real decision - designing the pipelines, modeling the warehouse, and shipping the dashboards on top.

🐍 **Python is my home language** - I use it for data engineering, analysis, automation, and backend development every day.

🏗️ I build **ELT/ETL data pipelines, dimensional data models, and governed datasets** that feed cloud data warehouses and BI tools.

📈 As a **Business/Data Analyst** I translate ambiguous business questions into KPIs, metrics, and self-service dashboards that leaders actually use.

☁️ I architect **data solutions on AWS** - from data lakes and warehouses to serverless compute and APIs.

📱 **Going forward I'm diving into Android app development** with Kotlin and modern architecture.

- 🌱 Currently learning: **Android development (Kotlin, Jetpack)** and advanced data modeling
- 💬 Ask me about: **Data Engineering, SQL, Python, BI dashboards, analytics, AWS**
- ⚡ Fun fact: give me a repetitive report and I'll turn it into an automated pipeline
